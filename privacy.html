Quick Upload

Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Quick Upload is a private publishing tool that runs on its owner's own computer as part of a personal video studio. It publishes the owner's finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Instagram professional account and Facebook Page. This policy explains what data Quick Upload accesses, how it is used and stored, and how to remove it.

Data Quick Upload accesses

ServiceWhat is accessedWhy
YouTube (Google)Permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and read-only access to your channel (youtube.readonly).To upload the videos you choose and show which channel is connected.
InstagramYour professional account ID and username, and permission to publish Reels.To publish the Reels you choose and show which account is connected.
FacebookThe Pages you manage (ID and name), and permission to publish videos to the Page you select.To publish the videos you choose to the Page you pick.

Quick Upload does not read your existing videos, comments, messages, subscribers, contacts or analytics.

How the data is used

  • Only to upload a video after you select it and confirm the upload.
  • To show the status of each upload and a link to the published video.
  • Never for advertising, profiling, analytics or training models.

What is stored, and where

  • The connected channel, account or Page ID and name.
  • Access and refresh tokens. These are encrypted before they are saved.
  • A record of each upload: the title, description and hashtags sent, the upload status, and the link to the published video.

All of this is stored in a private database on the owner's own computer. Quick Upload does not run a cloud server, and no copy is kept anywhere else.

Sharing

Quick Upload does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Video files and their text are sent only to the platform you chose (Google/YouTube or Meta), and only to complete the upload you confirmed.

Google API Services

Quick Upload's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Quick Upload uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

  • In Quick Upload: choose Disconnect for a platform. Quick Upload revokes its access with that platform and deletes the stored tokens.
  • Google: remove Quick Upload at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections.
  • Instagram: Settings, then Apps and websites.
  • Facebook: Settings, then Business integrations.

Deleting a project in the studio also deletes its upload records. Videos already published stay on the platform until you delete them there.

Children

Quick Upload is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
