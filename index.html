Quick Upload

A private publishing tool that sends your own finished videos to your own social accounts, using the title, description and hashtags you have already written.

  • YouTube
  • Instagram
  • Facebook Pages

What it does

  • Connects to your YouTube channel, Instagram professional account or Facebook Page after you sign in and approve access.
  • Uploads only a video you choose, and only after you review and confirm the upload.
  • Shows the progress and result of each upload, with a link to the published video.

Who it is for

Quick Upload is part of a personal video studio that runs on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts to other people.

Your data

Quick Upload uses access to your accounts only to publish the videos you choose. It does not sell, share or advertise with your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
